Customer Experience

What Homeowners Should Expect From a Plumbing Service

Good plumbing service is about more than the repair. These are the standards worth expecting from any plumber who works in your home.

Clear Communication

You should know who is coming, roughly when, and what they’ll look at. Updates should come before you have to ask.

Respect for the Property

Shoe covers, drop cloths and a clean work area are basic courtesies — especially in kitchens, bathrooms and finished spaces.

Understanding Repair Options

A good plumber explains what’s wrong in plain language and lays out the options, including when a repair is enough and when replacement makes more sense.

Transparent Service Discussions

Pricing, scope and any trade-offs should be discussed before work begins, with no pressure to decide on the spot.

Urgent Problems

Facing an Urgent Plumbing Problem?

Water leaks and plumbing failures can cause property damage quickly. Learn the immediate safety steps to consider and how to contact a qualified local plumbing professional.

View Emergency Plumbing Guidance

  • Shut off the water

    Close the fixture’s shutoff valve, or the main water shutoff if the leak can’t be isolated. Main valves are often near where the line enters the house, in the garage or in a box near the street.

  • Keep away from electricity

    Don’t touch outlets, cords or appliances near standing water. Only switch off a breaker if you can reach the panel without standing in water.

  • Protect the water heater

    If the water supply is off for a while, switch off an electric water heater at the breaker, or set a gas unit’s control to “off” or “pilot.”
