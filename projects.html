Kitchen Plumbing
Austin, TX

Kitchen Sink Pipe Repair

Problem
A slow drip under the kitchen sink had softened the cabinet floor. The P-trap connection was loose and the dishwasher drain line had a cracked fitting.
Solution
Replaced the P-trap assembly and dishwasher drain connection, installed new quarter-turn shutoff valves and braided supply lines, and tested every joint under full flow.
Bathroom Plumbing
Round Rock, TX

Shower Fixture Replacement

Problem
An original shower valve was dripping constantly and temperature control had become unpredictable during a bathroom update.
Solution
Replaced the valve body behind the wall with a pressure-balancing valve, installed new trim, a rain head and a slide-bar hand shower, then checked temperature and flow.
Water Heaters
Cedar Park, TX

Water Heater Replacement

Problem
A 12-year-old tank water heater was producing rusty water and leaving a puddle around its base.
Solution
Removed the failing tank, installed a properly sized replacement with a new drain pan, expansion tank and relief valve discharge line, then flushed and tested the system.
Pipe Replacement
Georgetown, TX

Exterior Water Line Repair

Problem
A soggy patch in the front yard and a rising water bill pointed to a leak in the water service line between the meter and the house.
Solution
Located the leak, excavated the damaged section, replaced the failed pipe and fittings, restored service and backfilled the area.
Drain Systems
Pflugerville, TX

Kitchen Drain Line Cleaning

Problem
A kitchen sink kept backing up despite repeated plunging, with gurgling coming from the dishwasher.
Solution
Cleared grease buildup from the branch line with a drain machine, cleaned the trap and shared practical habits to keep the line flowing.
Fixture Installation
Leander, TX

Bathroom Faucet & Sink Upgrade

Problem
Builder-grade faucets were dripping, and the homeowners wanted new undermount sinks and widespread faucets as part of a vanity update.
Solution
Installed new sinks and faucets, replaced the shutoff valves and supply lines, adjusted the drain assemblies and sealed every connection.
Commercial Plumbing
Austin, TX

Commercial Restroom Plumbing

Problem
A small office building had running toilets, slow restroom drains and a leaking valve in the mechanical room.
Solution
Rebuilt flush valves, cleared restroom drain lines and replaced the leaking valve during off-hours to keep the office running.
Plumbing Maintenance
Kyle, TX

Whole-Home Plumbing Check

Problem
New homeowners wanted to understand the condition of the plumbing in a 15-year-old house before problems appeared.
Solution
Checked water pressure, the water heater, shutoff valves, fixtures and exposed piping, then provided a prioritized list of maintenance items.
